## Execution rules

- Complete tasks in order unless a task explicitly states that it may run in parallel.
- Do not start production work that depends on a feasibility gate until Task 3.8 records owner approval.
- Preserve the existing scripts, Streamlit dashboard, Heroku deployment, and historical artifacts until the parity and cutover gates pass.
- Do not commit or print credentials. Live feasibility probes must redact secrets and require explicit opt-in.
- Every data-changing task must preserve source provenance and provide a rollback path.
- Requirement references use `R<requirement>.<acceptance criterion>` notation.
- A top-level task is complete only when its implementation, specified validation, documentation, and demonstration outcome are complete.

**Amendments (2026-09-20, owner decision):**

- **Cost is no longer a requirement or a gate.** The USD 20 ceiling, forecasts,
  budget notifications, cost quotas, and gate F9 are removed throughout. Only
  runaway-loop caps remain (per-request tokens/iterations, per-import fetch
  limits), and they are reliability controls, not budget controls.
- **The 2023, 2024, and 2025 seasons are frozen.** They migrate exactly as the
  baseline holds them, and the baseline is the parity target. They are never
  corrected or backfilled from the live source, and the documented 2023 Meet 2
  gap stays a gap. **Live intake targets the 2026 season only**; an import whose
  scope resolves to a frozen season is refused.
